# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> *“Friends don’t lie.”* — Eleven

---

## 📺 Overview

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series created by the **Duffer Brothers**.  
It first aired on **Netflix** in **2016** and quickly became a global phenomenon.

> This show perfectly blends **80s nostalgia**, **supernatural suspense**, and **heartfelt coming-of-age drama**.

---

## ⭐ Why I Love It

- 🧠 **Amazing character development** – from Eleven to Steve Harrington
- 🎬 **Cinematic quality** – feels like a long movie
- 🎵 **Iconic soundtrack** – includes The Clash, Kate Bush, and original synth scores
- 👾 **Creative monsters** – the Demogorgon, Mind Flayer, Vecna

---

## 📅 Seasons Overview

1. **Season 1** – Will goes missing. Eleven appears.  
2. **Season 2** – New sister, Demodogs, and the Mind Flayer.  
3. **Season 3** – Summer mall horror + Russian secret base.  
4. **Season 4** – Vecna rises. Darker and longer episodes.
